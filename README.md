# 🎵 MoodTunes — Music & Weather Mobile App

> A mood-aware music recommendation app that combines your current mood
> and local weather to curate the perfect playlist for any moment.

---

## 📌 Overview

**MoodTunes** is a mobile app prototype designed in Figma that intelligently
recommends music based on two inputs: the user's **current mood** and their
**local weather conditions**. Whether it's a rainy day or a sunny afternoon,
MoodTunes finds the soundtrack that fits.

## 🎯 Features

- 🎭 **Mood Detection** — Users select or input their current mood
- 🌤️ **Weather Integration** — App reads local weather conditions
- 🎶 **Smart Playlist Curation** — Music recommendations tailored to mood + weather combination
- 📱 **Mobile-First Design** — Designed for an intuitive, clean mobile experience
